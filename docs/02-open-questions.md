# Open Questions

Mark each as answered by moving it to [04-decisions.md](04-decisions.md).

## Commitments (resolve first)
- [ ] What exactly was promised? Get the colleague to write down everything said to the Founding Circle: a site, a newsletter, and "more".
- [ ] What does "more" mean? Candidates: community space/forum, events, resource library, advocacy campaigns, social channels, member directory, merch/donations.
- [ ] Was anything promised with a date? Which items are hard commitments vs. nice-to-haves?
- [ ] Who at the Founding Circle expects what, and who signs off?
- [ ] Newsletter: who writes it, how often, which platform (Substack, Mailchimp, Buttondown, etc.), and is there an existing subscriber list?

## Scope: what are we making?
- [ ] What is the deliverable: marketing/landing site, community platform, content hub, or a phased combination?
- [ ] What is the first thing a visitor should do? (Join? Read? Sign up for updates?)
- [ ] Is there an existing brand (logo, name usage, fonts, the 🩵💜🩷 palette) or are we defining it?
- [ ] "PMOS" is used rather than "PCOS". Confirm intended terminology and how we explain it to newcomers.

## Audience
- [ ] Who are the primary users? (Newly diagnosed, long-term, undiagnosed, supporters/family, clinicians?)
- [ ] Age range, regions, languages, and accessibility needs?
- [ ] What does the "Founding Circle" want to see first?

## Pillars: Learn / Connect / Act
- [ ] **Learn:** who writes and reviews health content? Is there medical review? How do we show sources and "last reviewed" dates?
- [ ] **Connect:** forum, groups, events, direct messaging, or external (Discord, WhatsApp, etc.)? Who moderates?
- [ ] **Act:** what does advocacy look like in practice (petitions, campaigns, surveys, story collection)?

## Safety, trust and privacy
- [ ] Health-adjacent community: what are the moderation and medical-disclaimer requirements?
- [ ] What data do we collect, and what are the privacy/consent expectations (health data is sensitive)?
- [ ] Anonymity/pseudonyms for members?

## Tone and design direction
- [ ] How should it feel? (Warm and soft, bold and activist, clinical and trustworthy, or a blend?)
- [ ] Any reference sites or things to avoid? (Stigma-adjacent imagery, "pink and flowers" clichés, etc.)
- [ ] Accessibility target (WCAG AA minimum?) and low-bandwidth/mobile-first considerations.

## Imagery
- Direction is photography-led ([D-001](04-decisions.md)). The questions below are now about how.
- [ ] Where do launch photos come from: commissioned shoot, Founding Circle members (with consent), or licensed? Photography is central to the "curated" feel we want.
- [ ] If members upload photos in the app: who sees them, how are they moderated, can people stay anonymous or blur faces, and can photos be removed?
- [ ] Consent and rights: who owns member photos, and what do we promise about reuse?
- [ ] Who is represented in the imagery, and how do we avoid stock clichés and body-focused imagery? PMOS can affect appearance (skin, hair, weight), so this needs real care.
- [ ] Photo guidelines: colour grading, framing and style so the whole site feels cohesive.
- [ ] Image performance and accessibility: weight on mobile, alt text, contrast over images.

## Practicalities
- [ ] Timeline, budget and who is involved (design, dev, content, community)?
- [ ] Preferred tools: Figma for design, any stack preferences for the eventual build?

## Donations
- [ ] Are we legally able to take donations? Organisation name, legal status (charity, non-profit, other) and who holds the money.
- [ ] Payment provider, currency, suggested amounts and one-time vs monthly.
- [ ] What donations fund. Don't publish a claim (such as "100% goes to...") we can't back up.
- [ ] Receipts, tax treatment, refunds and how to cancel a monthly donation.
- [ ] Privacy: what we store about donors, and whether donors can give anonymously.
- [ ] Tone: donating stays optional and never pushed (no pop-ups or guilt copy).
