Welcome to Cysters in Action 🩵💜🩷

## Why does Cysters in Action exist?

Because the experience of living with PMOS extends beyond symptoms.
People can encounter misinformation, stigma, delayed diagnosis, gaps in care and difficulty finding health information they can understand and trust.

But there is another part of the PMOS experience that deserves attention: community.

Cysters in Action was created as a space where people living with PMOS can come together to:

- ✅ **Learn** from reliable health information and shared experiences.
- ✅ **Connect** with others who understand parts of the journey.
- ✅ **Act** on issues that matter to the community and advocate for better awareness, understanding and care.

Our Founding Circle has taken the first step. Now, the listening, learning and building together starts.

**Learn. Connect. Act.**
