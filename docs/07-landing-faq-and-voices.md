# Landing Page: FAQ and Voices

Status: **added to the prototype (desktop and mobile), content incomplete.** Both sections depend on decisions we haven't made.

## Landing page order (current)
Hero → Why we exist → Learn / Connect / Act → Founding Circle → **Voices** → **FAQ** → Join (hive) → Footer

The Founding Circle comes first so visitors meet who started this, then hear from members, then get answers, then join.

## Voices
Real member stories with a photo, a short quote and a first name. Shows people like the visitor are already here.

- The prototype uses stock photos with **placeholder quotes**. No invented quotes: nothing goes live until a real member has agreed.
- Needs: consent process, what we say about reuse, whether members can use a first name only or a pseudonym, and a way to withdraw.
- Related: Imagery and consent questions in [02-open-questions.md](02-open-questions.md).
