Why Cysters in Action exists.

Because the experience of living with PMOS extends beyond symptoms.

The reason

People living with PMOS can run into misinformation, stigma, delayed diagnosis, gaps in care and difficulty finding health information they can understand and trust.

But there is another part of the PMOS experience that deserves attention: community. Cysters in Action is a space where people living with PMOS can come together.
