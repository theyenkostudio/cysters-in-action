What we care about

The problems people living with PMOS run into.

Misinformation
Health information that is wrong or misleading, and hard to tell apart from the good.
Stigma
Judgement and assumptions that make it harder to speak up and get support.
Delayed diagnosis
Long waits before PMOS is recognised, and a lot of time spent not knowing what is going on.
Gaps in care
Care that doesn't match what people living with PMOS actually need.
Information you can trust
Health information that is hard to find, hard to understand and hard to trust.
