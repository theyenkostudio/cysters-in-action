Why we exist

Living with PMOS goes beyond symptoms. Another part of the experience deserves attention too: community.

Misinformation. Stigma. Delayed diagnosis. Gaps in care. Information that's hard to understand and harder to trust. Cysters in Action is a place to come together around all of it.
